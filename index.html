FAQ

Good to know before we talk

What does Bhuvexa Consultancy actually do?+

We take a property through the full journey — assessing its potential, securing planning and statutory approvals (UDCPR, building permissions, RERA), coordinating development, and then marketing and selling it ourselves.

Do you actually sell property, or just advise on it?+

We sell it. Property sales and brokerage sit alongside our consultancy and approvals work — we handle positioning, marketing and buyer coordination for the properties we represent.

Do I need to have already decided what to build?+

No. Property assessment and feasibility are usually the starting point — understanding the land, the documents, the applicable regulations and the commercial possibilities before any planning decisions are made.

Can you help with building permissions and approvals?+

Yes — we assist and coordinate on building plans, development permissions and statutory approval procedures, including liaison with the relevant planning authorities.

Do you work with builders and developers, or only individual owners?+

Both. We work with landowners and property owners exploring their options, as well as builders, developers and investors who need planning, approvals, development and marketing support for their projects.

Is RERA guidance part of your services?+

Yes — we offer guidance and assistance relating to RERA registration, documentation and compliance requirements as part of our planning and approvals support.
