About the site

A weekend retreat with a valley view

Injole Panhala sits beside Masai Pathar, giving the site an open, elevated valley view that's hard to find this close to Kolhapur. It's built as a weekend plot and bungalow scheme — a place to slow down, not just a plot of land.

The project spans 6 acres with individual plots of 5,000 sq.ft, sized for a proper weekend bungalow with room to breathe — garden, parking, a place to sit outside. That plot size, this close to Panhala, is what puts it at the more spacious, luxurious end of what's available in the region.

Pleasant climate through all seasons, Panhala Fort and the surrounding scenic spots within a 2km periphery, and Kolhapur itself only around 20km away — close enough for a weekend, far enough to actually feel like one.

Half the project is already sold. If a plot here is something you're weighing, it's worth moving on sooner rather than later.
